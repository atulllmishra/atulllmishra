## 💫 About Me

Hey there! I'm **Atul Kumar Mishra**, a Computer Science undergraduate (B.Tech '28) and Full-Stack Developer from Jamshedpur, India.

My programming journey started with competitive problem-solving in **modern C++**, which ingrained a deep appreciation for algorithmic efficiency, clean abstractions, and systems thinking. Today, I channel that engineering mindset into the web ecosystem—building responsive, accessible, and high-performance applications with **Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js**, and custom **generative AI workflows**.

I believe great software should feel fast, effortless, and solve tangible human friction. Whether that means democratizing educational access with real-time multilingual AI video in **heyBuddy**, or bringing transparent bidding to IT procurement in **ProcureHub**, I'm driven by building software that actually matters.

- 🎓 **Education**: B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (2024–2028), Mahamaya College of Agricultural Engineering and Technology (MCAET, ANDUAT).
- 🚀 **Currently Building**: **[heyBuddy](https://github.com/atulllmishra/heyBuddy)** — An AI platform delivering real-time doubt solving and localized audio/video explanations.
- 🏆 **Achievement**: National Finalist at **IIT Guwahati ImpactHack 2025** with [Smart Agri](https://github.com/atulllmishra/smart-agri).
- 💡 **Core Interests**: Full-Stack Web Development, System Design, Applied AI / LLM Orchestration, and C++ Algorithms.
- 🤝 **Collaboration**: Open to open-source contributions, developer tooling, and innovative web engineering projects.
